    Put {query} where the search terms go.

    Tip: bangs work on engines that support them (DuckDuckGo, SearXNG…), e.g. !yt lofi or !gh startpage. Start with ? to force a search for something that looks like an address, e.g. ?node.js.
    Background

    The page draws no background, so your desktop shows through in browsers that support it (Zen with browser.tabs.allow_transparent_browser). Other browsers show their normal dark page color instead.
